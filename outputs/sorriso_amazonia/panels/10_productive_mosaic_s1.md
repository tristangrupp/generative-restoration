# 10_productive_mosaic_s1

**Sorriso / Lucas do Rio Verde** | CAR `MT-5107925-3BAF91A5F735426F9224DCFAF1BF3913` | Sorriso, MT | 5187 ha | 57.6 fiscal modules | Amazonia, Reserva Legal 80%

**Productive Mosaic**, version 1 of 3. The aim: keep the land earning while it complies. Agroforestry up to the legal limit, windbreaks along the field edges.

The law asks this property for 2755 ha. The plan grows 2745 ha of that on the farm and settles the other 10 ha somewhere else, which Art. 66 par. 5 allows: buy a CRA certificate, place a servitude on other land, or donate land to a conservation unit. 616 ha stays in crops and 1659 ha comes out.

The work: 1513 ha left to grow back on its own, 903 ha agroforestry, trees grown with a crop, 281 ha planted with native seedlings, 48 ha windbreaks and contour tree lines.

1826 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 9 mapped fields, 9 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 36% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 263 m from water and 315 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.85 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 98 separate patches, the largest 975 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 8779 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met partly by paying for vegetation elsewhere.

Faded colours outside the boundary are the surrounding land cover, off the same MapBiomas map as the current-state panel. Thin brown lines are the edges of the mapped fields.
