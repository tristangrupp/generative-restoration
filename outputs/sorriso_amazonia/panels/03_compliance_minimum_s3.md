# 03_compliance_minimum_s3

**Sorriso / Lucas do Rio Verde** | CAR `MT-5107925-3BAF91A5F735426F9224DCFAF1BF3913` | Sorriso, MT | 5187 ha | 57.6 fiscal modules | Amazonia, Reserva Legal 80%

**Compliance Minimum**, version 3 of 3. The aim: meet the law for the least money. Let land come back on its own where that will work, pay for vegetation elsewhere to settle the rest, and keep as much of the farm in crops as the law allows.

The law asks this property for 2755 ha. The plan grows 1109 ha of that on the farm and settles the other 1647 ha somewhere else, which Art. 66 par. 5 allows: buy a CRA certificate, place a servitude on other land, or donate land to a conservation unit. 2252 ha stays in crops and 738 ha comes out.

The work: 790 ha left to grow back on its own, 280 ha agroforestry, trees grown with a crop, 37 ha planted with native seedlings, 1 ha windbreaks and contour tree lines.

1826 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 9 mapped fields, 1 left whole, 3 gave up a strip along one edge, 5 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 63% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 118 m from water and 252 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.84 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 136 separate patches, the largest 793 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 7140 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met partly by paying for vegetation elsewhere.
