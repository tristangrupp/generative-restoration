# 06_water_first_s3

**Sorriso / Lucas do Rio Verde** | CAR `MT-5107925-3BAF91A5F735426F9224DCFAF1BF3913` | Sorriso, MT | 5187 ha | 57.6 fiscal modules | Amazonia, Reserva Legal 80%

**Water First**, version 3 of 3. The aim: look after the water and the soil. Put the strips along the streams in first and plant them so they close fast, then take the wet ground and the steep ground out of the rotation.

The law asks this property for 2755 ha. The plan grows 2748 ha of that on the farm and settles the other 8 ha somewhere else, which Art. 66 par. 5 allows: buy a CRA certificate, place a servitude on other land, or donate land to a conservation unit. 613 ha stays in crops and 1659 ha comes out.

The work: 2746 ha left to grow back on its own, 2 ha planted with native seedlings.

1826 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 9 mapped fields, 9 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 35% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 267 m from water and 316 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.86 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 95 separate patches, the largest 975 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 8777 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met partly by paying for vegetation elsewhere.

Faded colours outside the boundary are the surrounding land cover, off the same MapBiomas map as the current-state panel. Thin brown lines are the edges of the mapped fields.
