# 12_productive_mosaic_s3

**Primavera do Leste / Campo Verde** | CAR `MT-5107008-68B11B47FD234311B4ACF75DADE4C752` | Poxoreu, MT | 3315 ha | 55.3 fiscal modules | Cerrado, Reserva Legal 35%

**Productive Mosaic**, version 3 of 3. The aim: keep the land earning while it complies. Agroforestry up to the legal limit, windbreaks along the field edges.

The law asks this property for 102 ha. The plan does all of it on the farm and buys nothing elsewhere, restoring 207 ha in total, which is 105 ha more than required. 960 ha stays in crops and 128 ha comes out.

The work: 99 ha planted with native seedlings, 96 ha agroforestry, trees grown with a crop, 10 ha left to grow back on its own, 2 ha windbreaks and contour tree lines.

2148 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 50 mapped fields, 17 left whole, 18 gave up a strip along one edge, 6 lost a large part, 9 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 61% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 123 m from water and 64 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.86 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 79 separate patches, the largest 477 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 4305 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met on the farm.

Faded colours outside the boundary are the surrounding land cover, off the same MapBiomas map as the current-state panel. Thin brown lines are the edges of the mapped fields.
