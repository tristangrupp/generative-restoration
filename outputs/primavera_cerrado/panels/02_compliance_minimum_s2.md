# 02_compliance_minimum_s2

**Primavera do Leste / Campo Verde** | CAR `MT-5107008-68B11B47FD234311B4ACF75DADE4C752` | Poxoreu, MT | 3315 ha | 55.3 fiscal modules | Cerrado, Reserva Legal 35%

**Compliance Minimum**, version 2 of 3. The aim: meet the law for the least money. Let land come back on its own where that will work, pay for vegetation elsewhere to settle the rest, and keep as much of the farm in crops as the law allows.

The law asks this property for 102 ha. The plan does all of it on the farm and buys nothing elsewhere, restoring 185 ha in total, which is 83 ha more than required. 982 ha stays in crops and 115 ha comes out.

The work: 159 ha left to grow back on its own, 22 ha planted with native seedlings, 2 ha windbreaks and contour tree lines, 2 ha agroforestry, trees grown with a crop.

2148 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 50 mapped fields, 18 left whole, 22 gave up a strip along one edge, 5 lost a large part, 5 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 65% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 101 m from water and 59 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.85 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 81 separate patches, the largest 474 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 4275 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met on the farm.

Faded colours outside the boundary are the surrounding land cover, off the same MapBiomas map as the current-state panel. Thin brown lines are the edges of the mapped fields.
