# 07_corridor_network_s1

**Primavera do Leste / Campo Verde** | CAR `MT-5107008-68B11B47FD234311B4ACF75DADE4C752` | Poxoreu, MT | 3315 ha | 55.3 fiscal modules | Cerrado, Reserva Legal 35%

**Corridor Network**, version 1 of 3. The aim: join things up. Place the new vegetation where it links this farm's woodland to the woodland across the fence, and plant rather than wait, because a corridor does nothing until it runs unbroken.

The law asks this property for 102 ha. The plan does all of it on the farm and buys nothing elsewhere, restoring 395 ha in total, which is 293 ha more than required. 771 ha stays in crops and 273 ha comes out.

The work: 218 ha left to grow back on its own, 165 ha planted with native seedlings, 13 ha agroforestry, trees grown with a crop.

2148 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 50 mapped fields, 12 left whole, 14 gave up a strip along one edge, 12 lost a large part, 12 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 38% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 188 m from water and 78 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.84 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 74 separate patches, the largest 496 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 4470 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met on the farm.

Faded colours outside the boundary are the surrounding land cover, off the same MapBiomas map as the current-state panel. Thin brown lines are the edges of the mapped fields.
