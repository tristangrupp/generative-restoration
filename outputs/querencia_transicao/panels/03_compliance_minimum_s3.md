# 03_compliance_minimum_s3

**Querencia / Xingu headwaters** | CAR `MT-5107065-0ADD539B82204ADD923C937474D48855` | Querencia, MT | 4988 ha | 62.3 fiscal modules | Amazonia, Reserva Legal 80%

**Compliance Minimum**, version 3 of 3. The aim: meet the law for the least money. Let land come back on its own where that will work, pay for vegetation elsewhere to settle the rest, and keep as much of the farm in crops as the law allows.

The law asks this property for 3093 ha. The plan grows 1941 ha of that on the farm and settles the other 1153 ha somewhere else, which Art. 66 par. 5 allows: buy a CRA certificate, place a servitude on other land, or donate land to a conservation unit. 1574 ha stays in crops and 1701 ha comes out.

The work: 1228 ha left to grow back on its own, 645 ha agroforestry, trees grown with a crop, 60 ha planted with native seedlings, 7 ha windbreaks and contour tree lines.

1474 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 23 mapped fields, 2 left whole, 9 gave up a strip along one edge, 1 lost a large part, 11 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 56% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 133 m from water and 359 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.81 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 46 separate patches, the largest 714 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 6431 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met partly by paying for vegetation elsewhere.
