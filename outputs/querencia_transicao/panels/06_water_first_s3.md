# 06_water_first_s3

**Querencia / Xingu headwaters** | CAR `MT-5107065-0ADD539B82204ADD923C937474D48855` | Querencia, MT | 4988 ha | 62.3 fiscal modules | Amazonia, Reserva Legal 80%

**Water First**, version 3 of 3. The aim: look after the water and the soil. Put the strips along the streams in first and plant them so they close fast, then take the wet ground and the steep ground out of the rotation.

The law asks this property for 3093 ha. The plan grows 3083 ha of that on the farm and settles the other 11 ha somewhere else, which Art. 66 par. 5 allows: buy a CRA certificate, place a servitude on other land, or donate land to a conservation unit. 432 ha stays in crops and 2789 ha comes out.

The work: 3083 ha left to grow back on its own.

1474 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 23 mapped fields, 3 gave up a strip along one edge, 2 lost a large part, 18 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 41% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 220 m from water and 473 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.83 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 33 separate patches, the largest 838 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 7543 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met partly by paying for vegetation elsewhere.

Faded colours outside the boundary are the surrounding land cover, off the same MapBiomas map as the current-state panel. Thin brown lines are the edges of the mapped fields.
