# 12_productive_mosaic_s3

**Querencia / Xingu headwaters** | CAR `MT-5107065-0ADD539B82204ADD923C937474D48855` | Querencia, MT | 4988 ha | 62.3 fiscal modules | Amazonia, Reserva Legal 80%

**Productive Mosaic**, version 3 of 3. The aim: keep the land earning while it complies. Agroforestry up to the legal limit, windbreaks along the field edges.

The law asks this property for 3093 ha. The plan grows 3059 ha of that on the farm and settles the other 34 ha somewhere else, which Art. 66 par. 5 allows: buy a CRA certificate, place a servitude on other land, or donate land to a conservation unit. 456 ha stays in crops and 2749 ha comes out.

The work: 1723 ha left to grow back on its own, 1015 ha agroforestry, trees grown with a crop, 277 ha planted with native seedlings, 44 ha windbreaks and contour tree lines.

1474 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 23 mapped fields, 4 gave up a strip along one edge, 2 lost a large part, 17 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 39% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 240 m from water and 479 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.81 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 35 separate patches, the largest 842 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 7576 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met partly by paying for vegetation elsewhere.

Faded colours outside the boundary are the surrounding land cover, off the same MapBiomas map as the current-state panel. Thin brown lines are the edges of the mapped fields.
