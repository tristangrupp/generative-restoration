# 07_corridor_network_s1

**Querencia / Xingu headwaters** | CAR `MT-5107065-0ADD539B82204ADD923C937474D48855` | Querencia, MT | 4988 ha | 62.3 fiscal modules | Amazonia, Reserva Legal 80%

**Corridor Network**, version 1 of 3. The aim: join things up. Place the new vegetation where it links this farm's woodland to the woodland across the fence, and plant rather than wait, because a corridor does nothing until it runs unbroken.

The law asks this property for 3093 ha. The plan does all of it on the farm and buys nothing elsewhere, restoring 3136 ha in total, which is 43 ha more than required. 378 ha stays in crops and 2838 ha comes out.

The work: 3136 ha left to grow back on its own.

1474 ha of vegetation was already standing, in pale green. The plan keeps it and claims no credit for putting it there.

Fields: of the 23 mapped fields, 3 gave up a strip along one edge, 1 lost a large part, 19 went out of production entirely.

Where it sits: 40% within 100 m of a stream, averaging 217 m from water and 445 m from vegetation that was already there. The edges score 0.82 for running along the contour, against 1.0 for dead on it and 0.64 for ignoring the ground.

The area ends up with 33 separate patches, the largest 848 ha. Scored as one joined-up habitat they come to 7633 ha, and that number climbs as the patches touch.

APP is fully met. Reserva Legal is met on the farm.

Faded colours outside the boundary are the surrounding land cover, off the same MapBiomas map as the current-state panel. Thin brown lines are the edges of the mapped fields.
